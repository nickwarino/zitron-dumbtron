# Zitron Dumbtron

A source-heavy, interactive audit of Ed Zitron's writing about AI, 2020 to 2026. Every dated claim is scored two ways: how correct it is, and how much rides on it.

**Live site:** https://nickwarino.github.io/zitron-dumbtron/

- 610 claims from 110 posts, each tagged by scope, stakes (S1 to S7), distinctiveness, and a sourced verdict.
- 12 recurring-claim timelines, 6 adversarially-checked arguments, a price analysis with a linear/log chart, a reliability and human-baseline deep-dive, and a measured counterweight from Benedict Evans.
- 75 inline citations, each verified against its source by an anti-hallucination checker.

See `PROJECT.md` for the full methodology and an honest "where to distrust this."

Criticism and commentary. Quotations are used for analysis. Not affiliated with Ed Zitron or his publications.
